Delete Your Account & Data

Fenvo — developed by Cloud Continuum / Mohamed Magdy

This page explains how to request deletion of your Fenvo account and the data associated with it. If you have any questions, contact us at mohamed.magdy@cloudcontinuum.com.

Option 1 — Delete your account from inside the app

The fastest way to delete your account and all associated data:

  1. Open the Fenvo app.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Account.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm when prompted. Your account and data will be permanently deleted.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email mohamed.magdy@cloudcontinuum.com from the email address linked to your Fenvo account, with the subject line “Delete my account.” We will verify your identity and process the deletion within 30 days.

What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

  • Your account profile and login credentials
  • Your budgets, categories, and financial goals
  • Your transactions and any records you entered
  • App preferences and settings tied to your account

What data is kept, and for how long

We do not retain any of your data after deletion; everything is removed immediately.”
